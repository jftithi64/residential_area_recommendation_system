    The dataset has no street address, floor, building age or contact details, so each result is a lead to verify, not a confirmed vacancy. A rent far below fair is more often missing information than a bargain. Commute is a rough distance between area centres.

    How the model was tested

    Three models were trained on 15,242 unique listings (28,800 rows before removing duplicates) and tested on 20% of listings they had not seen. The simplest model did best on average error, so it is the one used. Every listing's fair rent comes from a model that never saw that listing.
